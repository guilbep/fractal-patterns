# Speed, phase 6: Truchet, Sprigs, the Accent color

## What changed

Phase 6 adds:
- the Truchet kind (Line art) and the Sprigs kind (Themes),
- the Accent color role and the "On the accent" contrast surface,
- fill and accent layers in the canvas script and paint worklet exports,
- the Herbarium palette and a Truchet first-visit design.

All of it loads at start. Phase 6 adds no dynamic import.

## How to measure

Run `make perf`. It builds the site for production, serves it with `vite preview`, and runs Lighthouse (mobile) 3 times per page. The tables give the median.

Start JavaScript is the sum of the gzip sizes (default level) of the script and modulepreload files that each `dist/*.html` lists.

All numbers come from the production build, on one machine, on the same day.

## What the numbers show

Patterns loads 1.8 kB more JavaScript at start: the two generators, the accent layer and the Accent role. Studio and Builder load about 0.5 kB more (the shared generators).

Each page stays under the phase limit of 2 kB more than the previous phase. Patterns is close to it.

The scores stay at 98 or 99. The layout shift is 0.000 on the three pages.

The total blocking time varies from run to run, also on an unchanged build. These numbers do not prove a change in it.

## A possible saving for the final review

Sprigs and Truchet load with the other generators at start. Loading the Themes family on demand would move its code out of the start bundle.
